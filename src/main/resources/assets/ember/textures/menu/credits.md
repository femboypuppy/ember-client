# Menu wallpapers

The wallpapers are bundled at 2560 × 1440 and work offline. The distributed PNGs are format conversions of 2560 × 1440 center crops downloaded from the original photo pages; `_blur` and `_thumb` copies are generated by `scripts/generate_menu_assets.py`.

- **Nebula / Nebula Live:** “Dark blue purple abstract wallpaper” by **BoliviaInteligente**. Original: https://unsplash.com/photos/dark-blue-purple-abstract-wallpaper-37WxvlfW3to
- **Silk:** “A black background with a pink and blue swirl” by **Pawel Czerwinski**. Original: https://unsplash.com/photos/a-black-background-with-a-pink-and-blue-swirl-ZkzobNDayXo
- **Obsidian:** “Black curved fan-like stacked shapes” by **Philip Oroni**. Original: https://unsplash.com/photos/black-curved-fan-like-stacked-shapes-VfOGf5RWkeg
- **Summit:** “Snow mountain under stars” by **Benjamin Voros**. Original: https://unsplash.com/photos/snow-mountain-under-stars-phIFdC6lA4E

All eight images are offered under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license), which permits free use and distribution. Downloaded October 19, 2026. Creator credits also appear in the menu; the original photo pages are linked above.
